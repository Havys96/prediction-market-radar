# Legal And Safety Notice

Prediction Market Radar is a read-only public-data research dashboard.

It is not:

- a trading bot
- a betting bot
- a signal service
- a financial advisor
- a broker
- an exchange
- a wallet app
- a geo-bypass tool

## No Advice

The dashboard does not recommend buying, selling, betting, trading, copying wallets, or entering any market.

Radar Score is a research-priority score only. It is not investment advice, betting advice, financial advice, legal advice, or a guarantee of outcome.

## No Execution

The project does not:

- connect wallets
- request private keys
- request seed phrases
- sign transactions
- submit orders
- place trades
- execute bets
- bypass geographic restrictions

## User Responsibility

Users are responsible for understanding and following the laws and platform terms that apply to them.

Prediction markets and event contracts may be restricted or regulated depending on jurisdiction.

## Donations

Donations support open-source development only.

Donations are not payment for trading signals, betting advice, investment advice, order execution, restricted access, profit-sharing, or private recommendations.
