# Demo Script

Use this as the script for a 20-30 second GIF or short screen recording.

Prepared assets:

- `docs/demo-preview.gif`
- `docs/social-preview.png`

## Goal

Show that Prediction Market Radar is a local, read-only research dashboard for prediction-market public data.

## Recording Flow

1. Open the local app at `http://127.0.0.1:8765`.
2. Show the Radar tab.
3. Point attention to Radar Score, probability move, volume, and anomaly cards.
4. Show Whale Watch and the market detail panel.
5. Open the Wallets tab and show the wallet scorecard / Wallet Alpha area.
6. Open the Alerts tab and show local descriptive alert rules.
7. Open the Reports tab and show Markdown / HTML / CSV export options.
8. End on the safety boundary text: read-only, no wallet, no orders.

## Voiceover Or Caption

```text
Prediction Market Radar is a local dashboard for prediction-market public data.

It helps review market movers, anomalies, large public trades, public wallet activity, local alerts, and exportable research notes.

It does not connect wallets, request private keys, place orders, or provide trading or betting advice.
```

## Short Caption

```text
One local read-only dashboard for prediction-market public data.
```

## What To Avoid

- profit wording
- signal wording
- copy-trading wording
- auto-betting wording
- arbitrage bot wording
