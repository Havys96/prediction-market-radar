## Builder Community Post

I made a small open-source dashboard for prediction-market public data.

It runs locally and is read-only. It does not connect a wallet, ask for private keys, place trades, give betting advice, or bypass restrictions.

Current v0.1.0 includes:

- market dashboard with Radar Score
- anomaly detector
- 15m/1h/24h probability-change history
- public large-trade observations
- public wallet scorecard
- Wallet Alpha Ranking
- local descriptive alerts
- Markdown/HTML/CSV report export
- snapshot fallback

I built it as a research tool, not a trading bot or signal service.

Feedback on data sources, scoring, and UX would be useful.
